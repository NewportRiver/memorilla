# LITE-008X Experiment Protocol

LITE-008X is a frozen, forward-only qualification-limit experiment. It constructs four new content-bound groups of eight counterfactual pairs: familiar vocabulary/unfamiliar entities (A), unfamiliar vocabulary/unfamiliar entities (B), unfamiliar vocabulary/unfamiliar templates (C), and relational multi-entity interference (D).

The W-R4 ridge and minimum-norm projections were loaded without refitting. All teacher, projection, encoder, decoder, tokenizer and soft-prompt artifacts were frozen. Optimizer authorization was zero. Textual competence was evaluated before latent execution. Latent results are exploratory because every group had insufficient textual eligibility under the predeclared criterion.
