# LITE-008P Scientific Limitations

- The independent oracle contains four fresh synthetic entity pairs, a small sample.
- The successful result establishes bounded isolated latent conditioning feasibility, not generalized bridge or associative-memory learning.
- Textual embedding and answer-token initializations are diagnostics and do not count as independent latent learning.
- Capacity comparison used a short four-update pilot and cannot prove that two or four slots are inferior at convergence.
- Q4 transfer was measured separately and failed consistent direction transfer; Q4 RSS telemetry is unavailable.
- Full repository regression remains dependency-limited by system Python lacking `transformers`.
- No protected final holdout was accessed and no production state was modified.
