# LITE-008W-R2 Incremental Learning Feasibility

Historical functional cross-validation does not establish independent generalization. The fresh teacher attempt demonstrates that decoder-supervised teacher generation is the dominant operational cost: 126 update ledger entries were produced before the 300-second subprocess bound forced termination, with no publishable teacher artifact.

Recursive least squares or incremental ridge remains mathematically cheap after teacher vectors exist, using sufficient statistics and periodic numerical stabilization. It does not solve teacher-generation cost, and no production continual-learning implementation is authorized.
